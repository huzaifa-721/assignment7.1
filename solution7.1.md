I built a horizontal navigation bar using Flexbox as per the requirements. I created a header containing a logo on the left named FlexNav, navigation links in the center including Home, About, Services, Portfolio, and Contact, and a Get Started button on the right side. I applied display flex to the main navbar container which made all its children align in a row, and I used justify-content space-between to distribute space evenly, pushing the logo to the left, links to the center, and button to the right, while align-items center was used to vertically align all items in the middle of the navbar. I also applied display flex to the nav-links container with gap 25px to create spacing between individual links. For styling, I gave the navbar a dark background, white text, padding, and box-shadow, and styled the links with hover effects that change background and color, and styled the button with a light blue background, rounded corners, and hover transition. To make it responsive, I tested the layout by changing the browser width and added media queries: for screens below 768px the navigation links and button are hidden and a hamburger menu icon is shown, and for tablet sizes I reduced gaps and used space-around.
